AC699 is a novel, orally bioavailable chimeric estrogen receptor-α (ERα) degrader that induces proteasome-dependent ERα degradation via cereblon E3 ligase recruitment. Here we report findings from a first-in-human, phase 1, dose-escalation study of once-daily AC699 (100–600 mg) in patients with heavily-pretreated, locally-advanced/metastatic ER-positive/HER2-negative breast cancer (ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: NCT05654532).
